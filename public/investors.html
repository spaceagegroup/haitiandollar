Investor Verification & Eligibility

Participation is limited strictly to qualified participants meeting verified criteria.

U.S. Accredited Investors (Rule 506(b))

Open to individuals and institutional entities qualifying under Rule 501 of Regulation D:

  • Individual income exceeding $200,000 (or $300,000 joint) in each of the two most recent years.
  • Net worth exceeding $1,000,000 (excluding primary residence).
  • Entities with total assets in excess of $5,000,000 or owned entirely by accredited individuals.

Non-U.S. Investors (Regulation S)

Available to international and diaspora participants residing outside the United States:

  • Not a "U.S. Person" as defined under Rule 902 of Regulation S.
  • Offshore execution with complete international KYC and passport authentication.
  • Compliance with local securities and digital asset regulations in the purchaser's jurisdiction.
