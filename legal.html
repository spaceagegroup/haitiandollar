Haitian Dollar Inc makes no representation that HTD will be legal or permissible in any particular jurisdiction.

Operational Risk

The functionality of HTD depends on the continued availability of third-party infrastructure, including public blockchain networks and mobile payment providers. Any interruption, failure, or delay in these services may temporarily or permanently prevent the redemption, transfer, or use of HTD.

Redemption Risk

While HTD is designed to be redeemable for Haitian Gourdes at the convention rate of 1 HTD to 5 HTG, redemption is subject to the availability of liquidity, the operational status of our partner network, and compliance with applicable law. Redemption may be temporarily paused if reserve levels fall below operational thresholds.

Technology Risk
