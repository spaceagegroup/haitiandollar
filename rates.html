Market Spread Disparity & HTD Value

Informal Cash Market Spread is ~4x Wider Than Commercial Banks

Unbanked individuals exchanging cash in the informal market face an average spread of 4.70 HTG (~3.5%), compared to 1.17 HTG (~0.9%) at commercial banks. HTD's 3% reserve redemption window and 0% P2P settlement eliminate predatory street cash friction.
